Switzerland — Core Entity
Stella Matutina Stiftung
Role
Holds the mission and charitable status. Employs staff, funds school operations, awards scholarships, builds the endowment, and the commercial subsidiary.
Decisions
Sets educational mission and curriculum. Hires and manages faculty and staff. Approves the annual budget. Governs the AG. Allocates endowment returns to operations and scholarships.

· Cantonal/federal registration · Swiss counsel · 2–3 months

Switzerland — Commercial
Stella Matutina
Role
Takes equity stakes in companies and projects founded by students and faculty. Returns all profits to the Foundation's endowment. May act as GP to a future venture fund.
Decisions
Evaluates and approves equity investments. Manages relationships with portfolio ventures. Governs any future fund structure. Determines timing and size of distributions back to the Stiftung.
To establish
Notarised articles of association · Supervisory board appointment · Intercompany agreement with Stiftung · 1–2 weeks
↓
Student & Faculty Ventures
Role
Companies and projects founded by Stella Matutina students and faculty. The AG holds minority equity stakes and receives a share of financial returns.
Decisions
Independently run by their founders. Operate outside the Foundation's governance structure.
↓
Endowment Pool
Permanent capital base
Role
Held and managed by the Stiftung. Grows through commercial returns and donations. Generates investment income that funds the school in perpetuity.
Decisions
The Stiftung board sets investment policy and approves annual distributions to cover operations and scholarships. Target: endowment large enough to make external fundraising optional.
Separately — Real Estate
Real Estate — Funder-Owned
Land Holding Entity
Role
Privately owned by funders. Legally separate from the charitable structure. Holds the school's premises and provides access to the Stiftung under a long-term lease.
Decisions
Funders determine the ownership vehicle. The critical decision for the school is securing a long-term, below-market lease with protections against sale or encumbrance without the Foundation's consent.
To establish
Long-term lease agreement with Stiftung · Structure depends on funders' jurisdiction · Negotiated timeline
